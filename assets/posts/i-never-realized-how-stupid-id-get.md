title: I never realized how stupid I would get.
date: 2026-07-12
private: false
---

well this is a fucking mess...

If you had told me years ago that we would have AI and that it would make me feel stupid, I would not have believed you. I always thought AI would unlock knowledge. I thought it would make learning easier, give people access to things they could never reach before, and let us understand more than we ever could alone.

I thought, to some degree, that automation was where AI would mainly go, and for the most part, I guess it has. A lot of things are automated now, but I am no longer sure whether they are being automated in the right way. Before, if I wanted to automate something, I would be the one designing the system. The automation would then go through stress testing and edge cases until it became coherent. These days, even the design stage is automated. The ideation is automated. The execution is automated. What remains of me is some husk that orchestrates what should happen, or spanks an AI with prompts telling it what it should stop doing. Even then, I often fail to create coherence in what I actually want.

Fuck. I do not know if there is a light at the end of this tunnel, but I think I still want to try making something.

I do not yet know what AI interaction should look like, only that it should differ from the current exchange. The chatbot-first model is undoing ideas that used to feel fundamental to user interfaces and user experience. We first recognize AI slop and feel disgusted by it, then it spreads, and after enough exposure it becomes normal.

I grieve the standards we had before.

Everything we did before was intentional. I really want to emphasize that word, because intention was what made the work ours. We created things because, for lack of a better word, we were expressing ourselves. What we made was us on a platter.

But if this is us on a platter now, then what are we? If you took away the AI, would there be any taste left on that platter? What amount of us would remain on it? Or would we simply be the title of it, a name placed over something that no longer carries much of us at all?

what a waste...
